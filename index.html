Using TrussLab 2D

Choose an example or draw nodes and connect them with members. Set the material and cross section, add supports and loads, then select Solve. Inspect a member to see its axial force and stress, or export your model as JSON to continue later.

What does the solver calculate?

The direct stiffness method calculates joint displacement, axial member forces and support reactions for a two dimensional truss with pin joints. Red indicates tension and blue indicates compression. The displayed deformation is exaggerated for clarity.

What are the model limits?

Members are straight and carry axial force only. The model assumes linear elastic materials and small displacement. It does not calculate bending, buckling, plasticity or dynamic response. A mechanism is rejected instead of returning a displacement result.

Does my model leave this browser?

Model calculations run locally in a Web Worker. Import and export use JSON files on your device. No account is required.
